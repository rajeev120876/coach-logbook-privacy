1. About this application

SBC Coach Log Book is an operational application for authorized railway maintenance staff and administrators to manage train journeys, coach inspections, controller and temperature readings, supporting photographs, staff nominations, approvals, and work reports. User access is controlled through employee credentials, PIN-based authentication, assigned roles, and device authorization. The application is intended for authorized work use.

This Privacy Policy describes the information the app accesses, collects, stores, uses, and discloses. Please do not interpret its use of railway-related terms or imagery as a claim of official endorsement unless the publisher is expressly authorized to make that claim.

2. Information handled by the app

Depending on your role and use, the app may handle:

  • Staff and account information: employee/PF number, name, designation, work phone number, email address (where supplied), assigned role, account status, login and verification status, session information, PIN-verification requests, device approvals, and administrative actions.
  • Device and technical information: a hashed app-specific device identifier derived from Android device information; connectivity and upload status; authentication and synchronization records; and app version information required for device authorization and troubleshooting.
  • Operational records: train and coach numbers, departure/arrival dates and times, nominated staff, temperature and controller readings, inspection entries, trip-close information, remarks, approvals, and associated timestamps.
  • Camera images: controller and temperature photographs captured in the app, including image identifiers, OCR-derived measurement values, and related capture details.
  • Precise location data: GPS latitude, longitude, accuracy, location time, and, where available, a location description used to add a location/time stamp to inspection photographs. Because the stamp is included in images, an uploaded image can contain location information.

3. How the information is used

We use these details to authenticate authorized users; apply staff, train, and device permissions; prepare and validate coach inspection records; capture or read controller/temperature data; add photo location stamps; queue entries while offline; synchronize entries and photographs; generate administrative reports; send verification emails; investigate errors; and maintain operational and audit records.

The app can synchronize pending work data in the background when network access is available. Device and location permissions are used for the relevant app functions rather than for advertising.

4. Permissions

  • Camera: to capture controller and temperature photographs.
  • Location (precise/approximate): to acquire and stamp the location of coach inspection photographs. A location foreground service may be active during the capture process and displays a system notification.
  • Internet/network status: to authenticate, download master and nomination data, upload inspection entries and photos, and synchronize reports.
  • Notifications, foreground services, alarms, and boot-related access: to support trip reminders, pending uploads, and scheduled synchronization, subject to Android permission and system controls.

Declining a permission can make a feature that relies on it unavailable. In particular, an inspection flow requiring a GPS-stamped photograph may not work without location access.

5. Local storage, security, and processing

The app stores account/session information, cached master data, work records, photos, and upload queues on the device to support offline operation and reliable synchronization. The reviewed version uses Android Keystore-backed encryption for certain local records and photographs and protected PIN-verifier storage. Network requests to its configured services use HTTPS. Access to administrative functions is role restricted. No internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

The app uses on-device text recognition technology to extract displayed instrument readings from images. Photos and related operational records may then be transmitted to the configured work-data backend.

6. Service providers and disclosures

Authorized administrators and personnel who are permitted to manage the operational system may view relevant staff, trip, inspection, photograph, and audit data. The reviewed app sends work data to a backend implemented with Google Apps Script and using Google Sheets and Google Drive for processing and storage. Google services may process relevant data as service providers under their applicable terms.

To prepare optional map/location stamps, the app can request map tiles from OpenStreetMap tile servers and may use Android's available geocoding/location services. Such services may receive network information (such as an IP address) and an indication of the requested geographic area. Where email verification is used, email addresses and verification messages are processed through the configured email service.

Information may be disclosed to other authorized recipients if an administrator exports, downloads, or intentionally shares reports. The app does not include an advertising SDK in the reviewed source, and the operator does not use work records for targeted advertising or sell them to advertisers.

7. Retention and deletion

Data stored on the device may remain while needed for offline access, upload retry, and app operation; uninstalling the app or clearing its data can remove local information but does not automatically erase copies already synchronized to the backend.

Uploaded inspection records, photographs, staff records, and audit logs are retained for legitimate operational, security, recordkeeping, and compliance requirements under the operator's applicable retention schedule. Records that must be retained for lawful or required operational purposes may not be immediately removable. The operator should publish or provide the applicable retention schedule on request.
